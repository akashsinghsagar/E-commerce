# LADDI — Premium Contemporary Indian Fashion

Live Website: https://e-commerce-kappa-rosy-10.vercel.app/

LADDI is a contemporary Indian fashion e-commerce website concept
celebrating traditional textile craftsmanship through a modern,
minimalist design. The storefront highlights curated ethnic wear,
handcrafted details, and editorial fashion storytelling.

Website Highlights
- Homepage: Editorial hero section introducing the brand and its
  seasonal collection.
- Shop by Category: Browse Kurtis, Co-ord Sets, Dresses, Festive
  Wear, and Everyday Wear.
- Featured Collections: Sections for New Arrivals, Bestsellers,
  and The Festive Edit.
- Brand Story: Content focused on Indian textile heritage and
  artisan craftsmanship.
- Fashion Journal: Editorial stories about Chikankari, capsule
  ethnic wardrobes, and natural dyes.
- Newsletter Section: An email subscription interface for
  collection updates and brand news.
- Customer Information Links: Navigation for order tracking,
  shipping, returns, privacy, and terms.
  
Design Direction
- Minimal, contemporary storefront
- Warm neutral colours and editorial typography
- Fashion imagery focused on garments, fabric, and craftsmanship
- Clear category navigation and collection-led browsing
- Brand storytelling integrated into the shopping experience
